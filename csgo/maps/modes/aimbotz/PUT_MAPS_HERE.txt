Maps for: Aim Botz (solo training)
==================================

Put the Aim Botz map here:
    csgo\maps\modes\aimbotz\training_aim_csgo2.bsp

Then an admin picks "Aim Botz (solo training)" in !gamemode, or types !aimbotz.

Getting the map (it is a Workshop map, not part of CS:GO):
  1. download_community_maps.bat tries varq.net automatically.
  2. If that fails: on a PC with CS:GO Legacy, subscribe to "Aim Botz - Training"
     on the Steam Workshop (id 243702660), start CS:GO once so it downloads, then copy
       ...\Counter-Strike Global Offensive\csgo\maps\workshop\243702660\training_aim_csgo2.bsp
     into this folder on the server.

The map's own buttons add the bots and change their settings (moving, armour,
headshots only, etc). The mode turns on infinite ammo, buy anywhere and no round
end; settings are in cfg\csgo_match\aimbotz.cfg (written on first start).

You can delete this text file.
